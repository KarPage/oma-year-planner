How to use the year planner

  • Click a day to select it. Click it again, then click another day, to add that range. Click the same day a third time to add just that day.
  • Drag across days to add a plan.
  • Drag a bar to move it. Drag either end to resize it. Double-click a bar to edit its note.
  • A one-day plan is a thin line at the bottom of that day. Several stack, and a small +N stands in for the rest. Hover lists them. Click opens the list so you can pick or edit one.
  • Drag the month names, or a year divider, to scroll the board. The wheel snaps to a month.
  • T jumps to today. N adds a plan. ← → move the focused day. ↑ ↓ move it by a month, or scroll the board when no day is focused. Shift+↑ ↓ scroll a month while a day is focused. Page Up Page Down move about six months. Home End jump to the ends. Enter edits. Esc closes. In the search, Esc clears the text, then blurs. ? opens this list. / searches. Ctrl+Z undoes.
  • The board shows this year and next. It can reach two years back and three years ahead when a plan falls in that window. Plans further out stay in your data and in search. Rolling 12 scrolls so the current month sits at the top. The year arrows scroll to January of that year.
