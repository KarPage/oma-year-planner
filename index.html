How to use the year planner

  • Click a day to select it. Click it again, then click another day, to add that range. Click the same day a third time to add just that day.
  • Drag across days to add a plan.
  • Drag a bar to move it. Drag either end to resize it. Double-click a bar to edit its note.
  • T jumps to today. N adds a plan. Arrow keys move the focused day. Enter edits. Esc closes. ? opens this list. / searches. Ctrl+Z undoes.
  • Rolling 12 scrolls so the current month sits at the top. The year arrows scroll to January of that year.
